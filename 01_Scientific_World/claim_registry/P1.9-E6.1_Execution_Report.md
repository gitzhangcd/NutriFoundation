## Core methodological decision

Scientific influence and scientific reliability are separate objects.

Journal-level metrics and citation counts may inform visibility, maturity and triage, but cannot independently increase claim reliability.

Reliability-relevant reception requires claim alignment, citation-context semantics, evidence-lineage de-duplication, and independent-replication assessment.

## Responsible-metrics rationale

The contract is aligned with responsible research-assessment principles: quantitative metrics should be contextual, transparent and not used as one-number proxies for research quality. It also adopts claim-level citation-context classification rather than assuming all citations are supportive.

Methodological anchors:
- DORA, Guidance on the Responsible Use of Quantitative Indicators in Research Assessment (2024).
- Hicks et al., Leiden Manifesto for Research Metrics, Nature 2015;520:429-431.
- scite citation-context framework: supporting / contrasting / mentioning-style contextualization with explicit classifier limitations.
