# P1.9-E6 — Batch001 Cross-Source Conflict, Redundancy & Evidence-Convergence Audit

## Executive result

The 20 F0 EvidenceUnits do **not** form 20 statistically independent evidence streams. The main scientific result of this audit is that the evidence ecosystem contains three different phenomena that must not be conflated:

1. **Independent convergence** — e.g. two distinct randomized diabetes-prevention trials.
2. **Evidence dependency / redundancy** — later meta-analyses, umbrella reviews, consensus reports and guidelines may include or rely on primary studies already represented in the registry.
3. **Apparent conflict created by scope** — differences in population, intervention, comparator, follow-up or outcome can make compatible findings look contradictory.

No same-PICO direct contradiction requiring forced reconciliation was identified at F0. Full-text F1 audit remains capable of changing that judgment.

## Major convergence clusters

### Cluster A — High-risk dysglycemia and diabetes prevention
EU002 and EU003 are independent randomized trials and converge on the conclusion that intensive multicomponent lifestyle intervention reduces incident T2D in high-risk dysglycemic populations. EU015 provides consensus-level support but is not independent evidence.

### Cluster B — Weight-loss diets and macronutrient composition
EU006–EU009 are primary randomized diet-comparison trials. EU011–EU012 are higher-level syntheses; EU015 is consensus guidance. Some primary trials show context-specific advantages, but the cross-source pattern does not support one universally superior macronutrient distribution or named dietary pattern for all adults. Population, diet quality, adherence and follow-up are major modifiers.

### Cluster C — T2D remission
EU010 and EU012 converge on intensive energy restriction / total diet replacement as an effective short-term remission strategy in selected adults with T2D. However, the synthesis likely includes the primary trial, so this is not independent replication.

### Cluster D — Mediterranean-style interventions
EU001, EU004, EU006 and EU011 show favorable outcomes in different settings, but the outcomes range from antihyperglycemic-drug initiation to CVD events, weight and HbA1c. This cluster supports context-specific efficacy, not a single universal effect.

### Cluster E — Carbohydrate quality / whole grains
EU013 and EU020 converge on favorable whole-grain/fibre signals, while preserving the distinction between trial-based intermediate outcomes and observational disease-incidence associations. Study-level overlap between syntheses is probable and must be audited before independence claims.

## High-value non-convergence / guard cases

### Surrogate outcome guard
EU005 shows that sustained weight loss and risk-factor improvement did not automatically translate to fewer cardiovascular events in Look AHEAD. EU004 shows that another dietary intervention in another population did reduce major events. The correct system behavior is not to choose one and reject the other, but to preserve intervention/population/outcome specificity.

### Observational causality guard
EU014, EU019 and EU020 support association-level claims only. The registry must block conversion into randomized causal effects without additional evidence.

### Guideline authority guard
EU015–EU017 are valid evidence objects for what organizations recommend and for applicability constraints, but they cannot be counted as independent causal-effect estimates.

## Redundancy risks

- EU007/EU008/EU009 may appear inside evidence syntheses represented by EU011/EU012.
- EU010 is probably represented in the remission evidence synthesized by EU012.
- EU013 and EU020 may share prospective cohorts.
- EU015 likely cites primary prevention and dietary-pattern evidence already represented elsewhere.

Until full-text citation-set de-duplication is complete, synthesis + primary evidence is encoded as a dependency graph, not summed as independent votes.

## Conflict classification

At F0:
- direct unresolved same-PICO conflicts: **0 identified**
- apparent scope conflicts: **multiple**
- probable evidence-dependency relationships: **multiple**
- causal-type mismatches requiring guards: **present**
- guideline/consensus authority dependencies: **present**

## Scientific consequence

ScientificClaim construction must operate on **evidence families**, not raw EvidenceUnit counts.

A claim with 5 references is not necessarily stronger than one with 2 references if 4 of those references are derivative syntheses/guidelines of the same primary evidence base.
