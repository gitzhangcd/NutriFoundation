# P1.9-E6 Execution Report

## Objective

Transform Batch001 from a collection of 20 F0 EvidenceUnits into a dependency-aware evidence ecosystem and construct the first ScientificClaim candidates without prematurely creating expert gold.

## Produced artifacts

1. `Batch001_Evidence_Relation_Map_v0.1.yaml`
2. `P1.9-E6_Cross_Source_Audit_v0.1.md`

4. `Batch001_Claim_Evidence_Matrix_v0.1.md`

## Audit result

No direct same-PICO contradiction requiring forced reconciliation was identified at F0.

The dominant structures are:
- independent convergence,
- primary-study / synthesis dependency,
- guideline / evidence dependency,
- apparent conflict caused by population, comparator, outcome or time-horizon mismatch,
- observational-vs-causal evidence-type mismatch.

## Candidate claims

11 ScientificClaim candidates were constructed.

High-value examples include:
- high-risk dysglycemia: multicomponent lifestyle intervention can reduce/delay incident T2D;
- no universally superior macronutrient distribution for weight loss across all populations/time horizons;
- intensive TDR/weight-management can produce short-term T2D remission in selected patients;
- surrogate/risk-factor improvement must not be assumed to reduce hard cardiovascular events;
- whole-grain/fibre evidence is favorable but disease-incidence evidence remains largely associative;
- UPF and meat/T2D records remain association-level;
- CKD and geriatric guidelines create explicit applicability gates;
- DIETFITS does not support the tested genotype/INS-30 biomarkers as useful diet-response selectors.

## Non-regression safeguards

- no observational association upgraded to causal claim;
- no guideline statement treated as independent effect estimate;
- no synthesis + primary paper double-counted as independent replication;
- no Mediterranean-diet evidence collapsed across heterogeneous outcomes/populations;
- no surrogate endpoint used as a substitute for hard clinical outcome;
- no candidate claim labeled GOLD.

## Remaining blockers before ScientificClaim GOLD

1. F1 full-text anchoring for claim-critical sources.
2. Study-level overlap audit for EU011/EU012/EU013/EU020 and linked primary studies.
3. Human/expert independent adjudication of high-impact claim wording and applicability.
4. Non-regression check against the ScientificClaim schema and Data Lineage Policy.
