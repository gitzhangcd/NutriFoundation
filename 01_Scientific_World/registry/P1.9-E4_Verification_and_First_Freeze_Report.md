## SA005 completion
SA005 is now Look AHEAD:
- PMID 23796131
- DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa1212914
- 5145 overweight/obese adults with type 2 diabetes
- intensive lifestyle intervention improved weight and several risk factors but did not reduce the primary cardiovascular-event outcome (HR 0.95, 95% CI 0.83-1.09)

This negative cardiovascular result is deliberately included to make the registry capable of representing competing outcomes and to prevent simplistic "weight loss -> cardiovascular benefit" transfer.

## Freeze semantics
F0 means the source-linked factual extraction is immutable for this release. It does **not** mean:
- full-text evidence coding complete
- GRADE certainty assigned
- ScientificClaim gold frozen
- recommendation approved
- expert DecisionEpisode gold created

A later F1 release may add full-text anchors and methodological details without silently overwriting F0 lineage.

## Full-text second pass
A PubMed Central full-text retrieval was attempted for rights-eligible records but the NCBI connector returned a rate-limit error during this run. Therefore no claim in F0 is labeled as full-text verified unless it was already directly supported by the abstract.
