## Why this second half was intentionally heterogeneous

SA001–SA010 are dominated by randomized intervention evidence. SA011–SA020 add:
- evidence synthesis,
- observational longitudinal evidence,
- guideline/consensus statements,
- population and disease-context shifts,
- causal-vs-associational boundaries,
- explicit uncertainty and heterogeneity.

This makes Batch001 suitable for testing whether AI Nutri can preserve:
Evidence != Recommendation != Applicability != Decision.

## Freeze semantics

All EU001–EU020 are F0-frozen. F0 means immutable source-linked factual extraction for this release. It does not mean full-text F1 verification, GRADE reassessment by AI Nutri, expert ScientificClaim gold, or DecisionEpisode gold.

SA015 has a rights-eligible PMC full-text anchor. The other new records are frozen only to the level directly supported by their PubMed abstracts unless later upgraded.
