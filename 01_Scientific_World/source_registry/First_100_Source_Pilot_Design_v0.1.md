# First 100 Source Pilot Design v0.1

## Objective

Construct the first scientific evidence pilot corpus for AI Nutri Data Foundation.

Target:

```
100 SourceArtifact
```

## Sampling Strategy

| Source Type | Target |
|---|---:|
| Guidelines | 20 |
| Consensus / Position Statements | 10 |
| Meta-analysis | 25 |
| Systematic Review | 10 |
| RCT | 25 |
| Cohort | 10 |

## Selection Principles

Sources should maximize:

- clinical relevance
- nutrition decision diversity
- evidence hierarchy coverage
- applicability challenges
- reproducibility

## Initial Domains

- obesity and weight management
- diabetes and glycemic control
- cardiovascular nutrition
- aging and sarcopenia
- kidney disease nutrition
- micronutrient deficiency
- cancer-related nutrition

## Expected Output

100 SourceArtifact

producing approximately:

- 500 EvidenceUnit candidates
- 300 ScientificClaim candidates

before verification.
