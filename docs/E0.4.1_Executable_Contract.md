E0.4.1 therefore introduces a deterministic semantic evaluation layer:


This layer is an evaluation sidecar. It cannot mutate EvidenceUnit_F0 or ScientificClaim truth.

## CanonicalSemanticForm

Canonicalization normalizes:

- biomedical aliases such as T2D, CKD, HbA1c, SBP/DBP;
- intervention aliases such as EVOO, TDR, low-fat / low-carb;
- dietary exposure aliases such as UPF, fibre/fiber, whole grain;
- effect-measure aliases such as HR, RR, OR and confidence interval;
- numeric syntax including percent symbols, decimal-middle-dot notation and numeric ranges;
- source identifiers such as PMID;
- semantic tags for observational, randomized, guideline, causality and applicability scope;
- polarity tags such as `not_causal` vs `causal_assertion`;
- age-scope polarity such as `excludes_younger` vs `younger_scope_positive`.

Canonicalization is deterministic but is not scientific Gold.

## Critical-field equivalence

Supported relations:

```text
equivalent
compatible_superset
partial
mismatch
missing
not_applicable
```

Critical fields include:

- kind
- population
- intervention
- exposure
- intervention_or_exposure
- comparator
- outcome
- effect
- recommendation
- applicability_boundary
- anchor

Equivalence uses:

- canonical concepts;
- field-specific informative concept filtering;
- numeric coverage for effect fields;
- source-identifier agreement for anchors;
- semantic conflict tags;
- polarity-sensitive applicability logic.

Generic low-information concepts such as `intake`, `diet`, `higher` or `adults` are down-weighted or removed before field-specific overlap is computed.

## Verifier numeric-direction repair

E0.4.1 identified a valid signed-effect representation:

```text
source: "reduced ... by 7.44"
candidate: "-7.44"
```

The verifier now permits the signed negative representation only when a local source-text context around the number contains a decrease-direction expression such as reduction, decrease, lower, fell or decline.

This does not globally erase numeric sign.
