## Interpretation boundary

Post-run audit identified two system-side confounders that must not be silently attributed to worker semantic failure:

1. deterministic verifier surface-form sensitivity:
   - `four` versus `4`;
   - `.20` versus `0.20`;
   - `.05` versus `0.05`;
   - `100 000` versus `100000`.

2. `kind` ontology ambiguity:
   - A0 requested a field named `kind`;
   - the Worker Contract and ResponseEnvelope template did not freeze an EvidenceUnit semantic-kind vocabulary;
   - the fresh worker therefore encoded study design labels such as `RCT`, `Cohort`, `Guideline`, and `MetaAnalysis`;
   - the downstream verifier/reference often expected semantic evidence kinds such as `intervention_effect`, `observational_association`, or `guideline_recommendation`.

Therefore:

```text
RawStrictBlindScore
!=
PureWorkerSemanticAccuracy
```

The raw report must remain immutable. Any verifier or ontology repair must be executed as a separately versioned post-hoc system-repair replay using the same frozen response bundle.
