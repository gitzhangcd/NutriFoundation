P0.1 converts the R0 requirement for independent expert Gold into an executable scientific governance contract.

## 1. Scientific ownership

\[
\boxed{\text{Gold}=\text{Independent, Source-Grounded, Adjudicated Scientific Reference}}
\]

Gold is not AB0 output, AB1–AB6 output, evaluator score, verifier/auditor judgment, Batch001 F0, or patient-specific decision reference.

Scientific ownership belongs to the Gold100 Independent Gold Panel. The Gold Custodian can store, hash, version, and release Gold, but cannot alter scientific content.

## 2. AB0 is comparator, not truth

Paper C compares workflow architectures, so AB0 is itself an evaluated workflow.

\[
\boxed{\text{Hidden Independent Gold} \rightarrow \text{scores AB0, AB1, ..., AB6}}
\]

Therefore Gold Expert ≠ AB0 Operator. P0.1 freezes person-level separation across the Gold100 confirmatory corpus.

## 3. Required roles

- Gold Annotator A: independent first-pass scientific annotation.
- Gold Annotator B: second independent first-pass annotation.
- Gold Adjudicator: distinct third role for any Gold-relevant A↔B disagreement.
- Gold Custodian: hidden-store and lineage custodian without scientific edit authority.
- AB0 Operator: human-only comparator, blind to Gold.
- AB1–AB6 execution/evaluation teams: blind to Gold until confirmatory outputs freeze.

## 4. Hard independence rules

1. Gold A/B and Gold Adjudicator may not serve as AB0 operators anywhere in Gold100.
2. They may not develop, tune, or operate confirmatory AB1–AB6 prompts, verifier, auditor, evaluator, routing, or escalation logic.
3. A and B may not communicate about a source before both first passes are locked.
4. A and B may not see each other's first-pass record before lock.
5. Gold annotators/adjudicators may not see AB0–AB6 outputs, scores, verifier flags, or auditor flags.
6. Gold may not be revised to improve observed workflow performance.

Thus the study separates Gold Production, Workflow Production, and Outcome Measurement.

## 5. Expert qualification

Each Gold annotator must demonstrate evidence-appraisal competence appropriate to assigned source types, study-design interpretation ability, Gold100 training completion, P0.3 calibration pass, and conflict-of-interest declaration.

A clinical license is not universally required for non-clinical scientific extraction. Guideline or normative sources require appropriately matched clinical/guideline-evidence competence. Adjudicator competence must match the disputed scientific domain.

## 6. Gold annotation object

The old schemas/expert_reference.yaml is not reused because it encodes decision-oriented concepts outside the R0 Paper C scope.

Paper C introduces paper_c/P0.1/Gold100_Annotation_Record_Schema_v1.0.json.

A Gold record contains Source identity/hash, version/evidence cutoff, StudyIdentity/dependency, one or more EvidenceUnits or a valid insufficiency state, claim type/causal level, source spans/provenance, source-level applicability boundary, critical-error opportunity map, and annotation/adjudication lineage.

## 7. Denominator independence

CSER depends on Applicable Critical Error Opportunities. Therefore the opportunity map is created during Gold annotation before AB outputs are used for scoring.

\[
\boxed{OpportunityExists \perp ABPerformance}
\]

Downstream model/workflow success or failure may not add, remove, or redefine an opportunity.

## 8. Adjudication

Any Gold-relevant disagreement requires adjudication, including StudyIdentity/version, EvidenceUnit boundaries, P/I-or-E/C/O scope, effect direction, critical numerics, claim type/causal level, recommendation normativity/exception, provenance/source spans, applicability boundaries, opportunity applicability, and criticality.

Allowed adjudication outcomes are accept_A, accept_B, synthesize_source_supported_value, insufficient_source, or unresolved_requires_protocol_escalation.

Unresolved critical disagreement blocks Gold freeze.

## 9. Agreement gates

Agreement is measured on locked pre-adjudication A/B records.

- Critical categorical fields: Gwet AC1 ≥ 0.80.
- Ordinal fields: weighted agreement ≥ 0.80.
- Numeric values: ≥95% exact/predeclared-tolerance agreement.
- Source spans: token F1 ≥0.85.

Failure means stop, repair codebook, recalibrate on non-Gold100 sources, version the change, and only then resume. Adjudication cannot be used to hide poor reliability.

## 10. Two-stage Gold freeze

Source-level state SOURCE_GOLD_PROVISIONAL requires locked A/B records, complete adjudication, complete source/hash/version binding, complete opportunity map, and zero unresolved critical disagreements.

Corpus-level GOLD100_FROZEN requires all selected sources to pass source-level freeze, all agreement gates to pass, Gold100 quotas/domain split to remain valid, strict-blind wall audit to pass, a hidden Gold package hash to exist, and the total applicable critical-opportunity count to be known.

If applicable critical-error opportunities are <600 before confirmatory unblinding, only the prespecified expansion rule may be invoked.

## 11. Amendment policy

Before confirmatory unblinding, semantic Gold amendment is allowed only for a source-grounded error discovered independently of AB performance and requires re-adjudication, new version/hash, and an amendment log.

After confirmatory unblinding, semantic Gold change for the current confirmatory claim is prohibited. A material discovered Gold error requires a new Gold/study version and, when necessary, invalidation or rerun of the affected confirmatory analysis.

## 12. Human-burden accounting

Gold annotation/adjudication time is not AB0 time and is not AB6 HITL burden. It is reported separately as benchmark-construction cost.
