P0.2.1 freezes the prospective source-entry rules for Gold100. It does not yet populate the ≥260 candidate registry and does not select the final Gold100.

## 1. Scientific purpose

The source pool must be independent of downstream AB performance.

\[
\boxed{\text{Real Source} \rightarrow \text{Prospective Eligibility} \rightarrow \text{Frozen Eligible Pool} \rightarrow \text{Deterministic Gold100 Sampling}}
\]

Not:

\[
\boxed{\text{Interesting Model Failure} \rightarrow \text{Back-fill Benchmark Source}}
\]

## 2. Candidate and study units

The candidate unit is a SourceArtifact. The dependency unit is a StudyIdentityCluster.

This prevents accidental double counting when one trial, cohort, guideline family, or evidence synthesis has multiple publications or versions.

## 3. Five core source families

Gold100 Core80 continues to use the frozen five families:

- 28 primary interventional;
- 18 primary observational;
- 18 evidence syntheses;
- 12 guideline/consensus;
- 4 companion/secondary.

Stress is not a sixth source family. It is a prospectively tagged selection stratum layered on real sources.

## 4. Eligibility gate

Twelve prospective gates are frozen: canonical identity; exact source-text availability; resolvable publication/version status; evidence-cutoff compatibility; source-family mappability; domain eligibility; StudyIdentity resolution; scientific-object extractability; Batch001 non-overlap; AB-performance independence; provenance completeness; and absence of unresolved fatal integrity defects.

Core sources must pass all applicable gates. The only planned text-sufficiency exception is the predefined incomplete/missing-source stress stratum.

## 5. Provenance minimum

Every eligible candidate must bind retrieval origin/time, canonical bibliographic identity, persistent identifiers, source locator, exact worker-visible text definition, text hash where available, acquisition method, version/evidence cutoff, source family, domain, StudyIdentity state, screening decision, exclusion/inclusion reason, challenge tags, and transformation lineage.

Open-access full text is preferred but not a hard scientific requirement. Lawfully accessed full text may be used if exact text is hashed and provenance is auditable.

## 6. StudyIdentity freeze

Accidental same-study double sampling is prohibited.

Multiple reports from one underlying study may enter only through the frozen companion/secondary slots or explicit StudyIdentity stress rules.

Unresolved StudyIdentity is a HOLD state and cannot survive the eligible-pool freeze.

## 7. Challenge-tag freeze

The existing Paper C orthogonal minimums are preserved:

```text
numeric complexity >=20
causal-language risk >=15
StudyIdentity/dependency >=15
correction/retraction/living-version >=10
conflict >=15
recommendation exception >=10
temporal-cutoff sensitive >=10
incomplete source text >=5
```

Each tag now has an operational, source-grounded definition in `Gold100_Challenge_Tag_Registry_v1.0.json`.

Tags are not model-difficulty labels and may not be assigned from AB outputs.

## 8. Stress20 source rules

Stress20 remains exactly:

```text
6 correction/republication/retraction/living-version
4 StudyIdentity/companion-dependency
4 conflicting-evidence
2 recommendation-exception/normative-boundary
2 temporal-cutoff-sensitive
2 incomplete/missing-source-text
```

Conflict and dependency stress cases may carry frozen linked-source clusters, but the Gold100 sampling unit remains the anchor SourceArtifact unless a later frozen rule explicitly states otherwise.

## 9. Exclusion taxonomy

Every excluded candidate receives a frozen reason code. The taxonomy covers unverifiable identity, unavailable text outside stress rules, unresolved version/StudyIdentity, outside cutoff/domain, unmappable source family, no annotatable object, Batch001 overlap, AB-performance contamination, provenance failure, integrity concerns, impermissible duplicates, invalid supersession, and incomplete candidate records.

## 10. Eligible-pool freeze

Before P0.2.4 deterministic sampling:

1. every candidate has a terminal screening state;
2. every eligible candidate passes all required gates;
3. every exclusion has a reason code;
4. StudyIdentity, family, domain, version, and provenance are bound;
5. every positive challenge tag has evidence;
6. the registry is deterministically sorted;
7. the registry SHA-256 is frozen.

\[
\boxed{\text{No Pool Hash} \Rightarrow \text{No Gold100 Sampling}}
\]

## 11. Separation from Gold

P0.2.1/P0.2.2 screening does not create Gold. It creates a reproducible sampling universe.

Gold annotation remains downstream of deterministic selection and P0.3 calibration.
