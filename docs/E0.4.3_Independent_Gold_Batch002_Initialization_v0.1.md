## Non-negotiable invariants

1. Candidate provenance MUST be recoverable to a real source.
2. Synthetic or invented literature provenance MUST NOT enter the candidate pool.
3. Retrieved evidence MUST NOT be treated as verified evidence automatically.
4. Batch002 candidate selection MUST NOT use downstream model performance.
5. Expert Gold MUST remain independent of worker/model outputs used for evaluation.
6. E0.4.2 frozen artifacts MUST NOT be rewritten.
7. Batch001 evaluator tuning MUST NOT leak into Batch002 Gold ownership.
